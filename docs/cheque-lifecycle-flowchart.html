How to Read This Diagram
A quick guide to interpreting the flowchart below
🔵
Rounded rectangles
State nodes — represent the current status of a cheque. Color indicates who owns that state.
🔷
Diamond shapes
Decision points — a role must choose one of several resolution options (e.g. Bounce resolution).
➡️
Arrows
Transitions — label shows the role (emoji) and the action taken to move to the next state.
🔁
Loops
Back-arrows indicate valid cycles — rejection loop and redeposit loop can repeat indefinitely.
🏁
Terminal states
Double-bordered nodes with no outgoing arrows. Green = success, Red = legal action, Gray = neutral end.
🏦
Lying With
Indicates physical/logical custody — who currently holds responsibility for the cheque at each status.
🏷️

Special Flows & Loops
Key behavioral patterns to be aware of
🔵 Rejection Loop
Accepted or Collected
↓ Accounts rejects
↓ Status → Rejected (back to Sales)
↓ Sales re-collects
↓ Status → Collected by Sales Person
↓ Can repeat indefinitely
⚡ Bounce Resolution
Cheque Bounced
↓ Accounts opens Resolution form
↓ Must attach supporting document
↓ Choose: Legal / NEFT / Redeposit
↓ Routes to respective terminal or loop
🔁 Redeposit Loop
Redeposited (resolution chosen)
↓ Accounts deposits again
↓ Status → Deposited
↓ May Bounce again
↓ Can be redeposited again
↓ Loop can repeat
💸 NEFT Path
Cheque is in Accepted state
↓ Customer pays via bank transfer
↓ Sales marks NEFT Received
↓ Accounts confirms payment
↓ Terminal: Cleared via NEFT
🔄 Replacement Cheque
Cheque is in Accepted state
↓ Customer provides new cheque
↓ Sales marks New Cheque Received
↓ This cheque → terminal (neutral)
↓ A new cheque record is created
📋 Cheque Creation
Sales person creates new cheque
↓ Fills: Customer, Bank, Amount, Date, Type
↓ Attaches cheque image (mandatory)
↓ Chooses initial status:
  • Collected by Sales Person
  • Pending with Customer

Status Reference Table
All 13 statuses — role, custody, type, and description
# Status Who Acts Lying With Type Description
01 Pending with Customer 👤 Sales 🧍 Customer ⏳ In Progress Cheque created but not yet picked up from the customer. Sales must follow up and collect it.
02 Collected by Sales Person 👤 Sales 👤 Sales ⏳ In Progress Cheque is physically with the Sales person. Must be submitted to Accounts for review. Entry point for Accounts workflow.
03 Accepted 📋 Accounts 📋 Accounts ⏳ In Progress Accounts has verified and accepted the cheque. From here it can be deposited, rejected, or resolved via NEFT/replacement/cancellation.
04 Rejected 📋 Accounts 👤 Sales ⏳ In Progress Cheque rejected by Accounts (e.g. discrepancy, incorrect details). Returns to Sales for re-collection. Rejection loop can repeat.
05 Deposited 📋 Accounts 🏦 Bank ⏳ In Progress Cheque submitted to the bank for clearing. Awaiting bank's response — will either Clear or Bounce.
06 Cleared 📋 Accounts 📋 HO Accounts ✅ Terminal Successful outcome. Bank has cleared the cheque. Payment fully recovered. No further action needed.
07 Bounced 📋 Accounts 🔒 Credit Control ❗ Action Needed Bank rejected the cheque. Accounts must resolve via Resolution form: attach document and choose Legal / NEFT / Redeposit.
08 Redeposited 📋 Accounts 📋 Accounts ⏳ In Progress Accounts chose to redeposit after a bounce. Cheque will be sent to bank again. May bounce again — redeposit loop possible.
09 Legal Notice Initiated 📋 Accounts 🔒 Credit Control ❌ Terminal Legal escalation. Accounts chose legal route after bounce. Case is now with Credit Control / legal team. Requires supporting document.
10 Cleared via NEFT 📋 Accounts 📋 HO Accounts ✅ Terminal Successful outcome. Customer settled the amount via bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS). Accounts confirms. Payment recovered without clearing the physical cheque.
11 New Cheque Received 👤 Sales 👤 Sales ⚪ Terminal Replaced. Customer issued a replacement cheque. This cheque record ends here. A new cheque record is created for the replacement.
12 NEFT Received 👤 Sales 🔒 Credit Control ⏳ In Progress Sales reports that the customer paid via bank transfer. Awaiting Accounts confirmation before marking Cleared via NEFT.
13 Order Cancelled 👤 Sales 👤 Sales ⚪ Terminal Order void. The underlying order was cancelled while the cheque was in Accepted state. No payment will be collected.
